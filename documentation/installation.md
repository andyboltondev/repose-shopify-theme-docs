# Installation

## Adding Repose to your store

1. From the Shopify Theme Store listing, click **Add theme**. This installs
   Repose into your store's theme library without publishing it, so your
   current storefront keeps running while you set it up.
2. In Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Themes**. Repose appears under
   your non-published themes.
3. Click **Customize** to open the theme editor and start setting things up,
   or **Publish** if you're ready to make it live immediately.

## Choosing a preset

Repose ships three presets, each a complete look built on the same theme
code: **Repose** (suited to home and interiors), **Selvedge** (suited to
clothing) and **Nacre** (suited to jewellery and accessories).

In the theme editor, open the preset menu at the top of the left-hand panel
to switch between them. A preset sets colours, fonts, spacing, card and
button shapes, and similar presentational choices, all at once. Switching
presets does not add, remove or rearrange sections on your pages, so it is
safe to try each one without losing any content you've already added.

Pick whichever preset is closest to what you want and refine it from there
using **Theme settings**, covered in the next page. There is no requirement
to keep every setting a preset chose. If you'd like to see all three presets
side by side before deciding, the **Theme demo grid** section (see
[Sections and blocks](sections-and-blocks.md)) can link out to example
storefronts running each one, where the theme's author has set that up.

## Setting up your storefront

With a preset chosen, a sensible order to work through is:

1. **Theme settings > Brand**: add your logo, a dark-mode logo if you have
   one, and a favicon.
2. **Theme settings**: adjust colours, typography and layout to match your
   brand. See [Theme settings](theme-settings.md) for what each control does.
3. **Header and footer**: set your main menu, footer menu and store
   description. See [Navigation and menus](navigation-and-menus.md).
4. **Home page**: add, remove and reorder sections to build your homepage.
   See [Sections and blocks](sections-and-blocks.md) for what's available.
5. **Product and collection pages**: check the default templates suit your
   catalogue, and look at the alternate templates if a specific product or
   collection needs a different layout. See
   [Products and collections](products-and-collections.md).

## Publishing

Once you're happy with how the theme editor preview looks, click **Publish**
in **Online Store > Themes** to make Repose your live storefront. You can keep
customising a published theme at any time. Changes in the theme editor are
only visible to customers once you save, and the editor always shows you a
live preview before that happens.

## Demo content

Repose's demo store content, images and sample products are for
demonstration only and are not installed with the theme. Your store starts
with your own products, pages and menus; sections that reference a
collection, page or menu simply show a prompt in the editor until you connect
one.
